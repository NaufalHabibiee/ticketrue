## Problem

Buying concert tickets online has three familiar pain points:

1. **Fake or duplicated tickets.** Scalpers photocopy or resell the same ticket to multiple people. By the time you realize, you've lost money and the concert is tomorrow.

2. **Scalpers who mark up 500%.** You pay $50 for a ticket; it's resold for $250. The artist and venue make nothing from the resale profit.

3. **No easy way to prove ownership.** "Is this ticket real?" requires trusting a centralized ticketing company. If they get hacked, you lose proof of ownership.

**Result:** Millions of fans miss concerts they bought tickets for, or overpay for scalped seats.

---

## Solution: Ticketrue

Ticketrue is a **ticketing dApp on BOT Chain** where every ticket is recorded on-chain under your wallet. This fixes all three problems:

### 1. One Ticket, One Owner
- When you buy a ticket, the smart contract issues it to your wallet address.
- The blockchain is the source of truth: one wallet = one verified ticket per concert.
- Impossible to duplicate or forge.

### 2. Fair Resale, Forever
- You can only resell a ticket at or below what you paid.
- The organizer gets 5% of every resale (not the scalper).
- No more price gouging.

### 3. Instant Verification
- Every ticket has a QR code. Scan it anywhere.
- Public check page reads from the contract in real-time.
- "Live pass" is signed by your wallet, valid for 5 minutes, so a screenshot is useless.

### 4. Gate Check-In (No Double Entry)
- Organizers can mark a ticket as "admitted" on-chain.
- Once checked in, it cannot be resold or re-entered.

## How It Works

### For Fans

1. **Browse & Buy**
   - Open https://ticketrue.web.id
   - Pick a concert (seated venue or tier-based pricing)
   - Select your seat or tier + quantity (up to 4 per wallet)
   - Pay in BOT (BOT Chain's native token)
   - Instant: ticket appears in your **My Tickets**

2. **Your Ticket Is Yours**
   - QR pass that proves ownership (scan anywhere, anytime)
   - Add to calendar
   - Download as image
   - Share with friends (they can scan to verify, but can't claim it)

3. **Can't Use It? Resell It**
   - List on the resale marketplace at or below your purchase price
   - Buyer pays, you receive funds instantly
   - No middleman, no fees between you and buyer (except 5% to organizer)

4. **At the Gate**
   - Staff scans your QR
   - Contract confirms you own it
   - You're in. Ticket is marked as "used" (can't resell)

### For Organizers

1. **Create & Configure Events**
   - Set price & capacity per zone/tier
   - Allow demo claims (testnet) or real payments (mainnet)

2. **Monitor Live Sales**
   - Real-time dashboard: sales, remaining inventory, resale volume
   - See which seats/tiers are hot

3. **Manage Gate Staff**
   - Add wallet addresses as gate staff
   - They can check tickets in on-chain

4. **Withdraw Funds**
   - All payments go to your wallet
   - Resale fees accumulate automatically
   - Withdraw whenever you want

## Why We Built This

Ticketrue started as a hackathon project (Build Week Vol.2, RWA track) to solve a real problem: **fans deserve a fair, transparent, fraud-proof way to buy and resell concert tickets.**

Traditional ticketing platforms take 20–30% in fees, resellers take 50–500% markups, and fans have no proof their ticket is real.

With blockchain, we remove all middlemen. The organizer, the fan, and the blockchain are enough.
